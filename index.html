Hermes

A private, self-hosted Google Workspace MCP deployment.

This is a single-operator deployment of the open-source google_workspace_mcp server. It lets the operator's own AI assistant read and act on the operator's own Google Workspace data — Gmail, Calendar, and read-only Drive, Docs, and Sheets.

It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to other users. There is no sign-up. The only accounts that can authorize it are the operator's own.

See the privacy policy for what the application accesses and how that data is handled.
