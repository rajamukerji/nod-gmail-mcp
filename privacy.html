Privacy Policy

Hermes — personal Google Workspace MCP deployment. Effective 24 November 2026.

Who this covers

This application is operated by one individual for their own personal use. It has no other users, no sign-up, and collects nothing from visitors to this page.

What it accesses

When the operator authorizes the application against their own Google Account, it may access, on that account only:

  • Gmail — read, compose, send, and label messages
  • Google Calendar — read and manage events
  • Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets — read-only
  • Basic profile and email address, to identify which account a request belongs to

How data is handled

  • Google data is fetched on demand to answer the operator's own requests. It is not collected into a separate database, profiled, or analyzed in bulk.
  • OAuth tokens are stored on the operator's own private server, readable only by the account running the service. They are never shared.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and none is used to train any machine-learning model.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the features above, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked. The operator can revoke access at any time at Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps, which immediately ends the application's access.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
